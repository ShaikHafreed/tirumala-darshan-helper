# Store listing text (Edge Add-ons)

**Name:** Tirumala Darshan Helper
**Short description:** Saves pilgrim details privately and fills text boxes on the TTD form when you ask. Release-time reminders on PC and phone.
**Description:**
Tirumala Darshan Helper is an unofficial, free helper for people booking TTD Tirumala darshan online. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TTD.
- Keeps pilgrim details on your computer, encrypted with your PIN. ID numbers are saved only if you choose.
- Fills the Name, Age and ID number text boxes of the Pilgrim Details form after you press a button and check a preview. You do login, OTP, queue, dropdowns, payment.
- Reminders 10 min, 5 min and at release time, on your PC and (optionally) your phone through the free ntfy app.
- Booking-day checklist, health check, practice page, English / Telugu / Hindi / Tamil.
No tracking, no ads, no account on any server.
**Permission justification:** activeTab + scripting: type into the form of the tab you are on after you click. alarms + notifications: reminders. storage: save your profile locally. Optional ntfy.sh access: phone alerts, requested only when you switch them on.
**Single purpose:** help a user prepare and fill their own pilgrim details on the TTD booking form and remind them of the release time.
**Screenshots:** use only fake names and fake numbers (tools/ can generate the demo page screenshots from mock.html).
